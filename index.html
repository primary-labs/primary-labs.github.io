For teachers

Use it on the classroom screen

The letters and pictures are big enough to see from the back of the room, and the buttons are easy to tap on a smartboard or tablet. It also works with a keyboard.

Record your own voice

In Letter Chains, record each sound and word once, and the lab plays your recordings instead of the built-in audio.

Saved on your device

Recordings and custom chains are stored in your browser. We don't collect any data, and there are no ads.
