For teachers

Each lab fits a five-minute routine you can run with the whole class, a small group, or a center.

Use it on the classroom screen

Large letters and big tap targets work on projectors, interactive boards, and tablets. Students can also use the keyboard.

Record your own voice

Record each sound and word once. Children then hear the same model you use in your lessons.

Nothing leaves your browser

There are no accounts, ads, or tracking. Recordings and custom chains are saved only on your device.
